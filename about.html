About the doctor

Meet Dr. Ismaila Kishimi

A dermatosurgeon who explains every diagnosis and treatment in plain language.

Certified skin nerd

Dr. Ismaila Kishimi is a medical doctor (MBBS) from the Faculty of Medicine, Ahmadu Bello University, with a postgraduate diploma in family medicine from the National Postgraduate Medical College of Nigeria. He earned an MSc in Dermatology and Dermatosurgery at the Institute of Dermatology, Bangkok, Thailand, where he spent three years training in dermatology, dermatosurgery and aesthetic techniques, and went on to fellowship training in allergic and occupational dermatology and in laser and cutaneous surgery at the same institute.

At Zmedspa, the team treats conditions of the skin, hair and nails and explains every diagnosis and treatment plan in plain language, so you know what to expect.

Training and qualifications

  • MBBS, Faculty of Medicine, Ahmadu Bello University
  • Postgraduate diploma in family medicine, National Postgraduate Medical College of Nigeria
  • MSc in Dermatology and Dermatosurgery, Institute of Dermatology, Bangkok, Thailand (three years of training in dermatology, dermatosurgery and aesthetic techniques)
  • Fellowship training in allergic and occupational dermatology, and in laser and cutaneous surgery, Institute of Dermatology, Bangkok

Areas of practice

  • Clinical dermatology for skin, hair and nails
  • Dermatosurgery, biopsies and nail surgery
  • Scar and keloid treatment
  • Laser and hair removal
  • Chemical peels and aesthetic medicine
  • Allergic and occupational dermatology

How we work

Every visit begins with listening. We examine the problem, explain what we find, and agree a plan with you. You should leave knowing what is wrong, what the treatment involves and when to come back.
