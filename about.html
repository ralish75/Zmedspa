About me

Meet Dr. Ismaila Kishim

A dermatologist who explains your diagnosis and treatment in plain language.

I am a dermatologist with an MSc in Dermatology and Dermatosurgery. I treat conditions of the skin, hair and nails, and I explain every diagnosis and treatment plan in plain language so you know what to expect.

Training and fellowship

  • MD, Doctor of Medicine
  • MSc in Dermatology and Dermatosurgery
  • Clinical fellow in allergic and occupational dermatology, laser and cutaneous surgery at the Institute of Dermatology, Bangkok, Thailand

Areas of practice

  • Medical dermatology for skin, hair and nails
  • Aesthetic medicine
  • Laser treatments and hair removal
  • Cutaneous (skin) surgery
  • Allergic and occupational dermatology

How I work

Every visit begins with listening. I examine the problem, explain what I find, and agree a plan with you. You should leave knowing what is wrong, what the treatment involves and when to come back.
