Eczema: everyday skin care that makes a difference

Simple daily habits that calm dry, itchy skin and reduce flare-ups.

Draft text for Dr. Kishim to review and approve before the site goes live.

← All articles

Eczema makes the skin dry, itchy and easily irritated. It cannot always be cured, but good daily care can keep it under control.

Daily care

  • Moisturise at least once a day, ideally straight after bathing
  • Use lukewarm water and a gentle, fragrance-free cleanser
  • Avoid known triggers such as harsh soaps, some fabrics and sweat left on the skin
  • Keep nails short to limit damage from scratching

When to see a dermatologist

  • The skin is weeping, cracked, or looks infected
  • Itching is disturbing your sleep
  • The rash keeps coming back, especially on the hands, which may point to an allergy or a work-related cause

Please do not use strong steroid creams for long periods without medical advice, as they can thin the skin.
