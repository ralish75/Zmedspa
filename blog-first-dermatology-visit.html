What to expect at your first dermatology visit

How a consultation works, what to bring, and the questions worth asking.

Draft text for Dr. Kishim to review and approve before the site goes live.

← All articles

A first visit is mainly a conversation. I want to understand your problem, how long you have had it, and what you have already tried, before I examine your skin, hair or nails.

What to bring

  • The names of any creams, tablets or supplements you use, or the products themselves
  • Earlier test results or reports, if you have them
  • A list of your questions
  • Photos of how the problem looked when it was at its worst, if it comes and goes

During the consultation

I ask about your health, allergies and work, then examine the affected area. Sometimes I need a small test, such as a skin scraping or a patch test, to be sure of the diagnosis.

Afterwards

I explain what I found in plain language, set out the treatment plan, and tell you what improvement to expect and when to come back. Please ask if anything is unclear.

This article is general information and does not replace a consultation.

Ready to book?

Choose an in-person visit at the clinic or an online consultation, and I will confirm a time with you on WhatsApp.
