Conditions

Common conditions, in plain language

A short guide to what I see most often, and when it is worth booking a visit.

Draft text for Dr. Kishim to review and approve before the site goes live.
Skin

Acne and acne scars

Blocked, inflamed pores that cause spots, deep lumps and sometimes marks or scars.

See a doctor if: Spots that are painful, scarring, or not improving with simple care.

Related treatments → Skin

Eczema and dermatitis

Dry, itchy, inflamed skin that can flare up and settle again.

See a doctor if: Weeping or cracked skin, poor sleep from itching, or a rash that keeps returning.

Related treatments → Skin

Psoriasis

Thick, scaly, well-defined patches, often on the elbows, knees and scalp.

See a doctor if: Patches that spread, crack, or affect the joints or nails.

Related treatments → Skin

Fungal skin infections

Itchy, ring-shaped or scaly patches caused by fungus, such as ringworm.

See a doctor if: A rash that is spreading or has not cleared after a short course of home treatment.

Related treatments → Hair

Hair loss

Thinning or shedding that can have many causes, from genes to stress to scalp disease.

See a doctor if: Sudden shedding, bald patches, or thinning that worries you.

Related treatments → Hair

Dandruff and scalp conditions

Flaking, itching or redness of the scalp.

See a doctor if: Flaking with redness, sores or hair loss.

Related treatments → Nails

Fungal nail infection

Thickened, discoloured or crumbly nails, most often on the toes.

See a doctor if: Changes to the nail that are spreading or painful.

Related treatments → Allergy

Contact allergy

A rash triggered by something that touches the skin, such as a metal, cosmetic or work material.

See a doctor if: A rash on the hands or face that keeps returning without a clear cause.

Related treatments → Allergy

Hand eczema

Dry, cracked, itchy hands, often linked to wet work or irritants.

See a doctor if: Cracks that bleed, or hand problems that affect your work.
