Acne is common and treatable. It happens when pores become blocked and inflamed, and it can leave marks or scars if it is not treated well.

## Habits that help

- Wash your face gently twice a day with a mild cleanser
- Avoid picking or squeezing spots, which raises the risk of scarring
- Choose products labelled non-comedogenic
- Be patient. Most treatments take several weeks to show a result

## When to see a dermatologist

- The acne is painful, deep or leaving scars or dark marks
- Over-the-counter products have not helped after a couple of months
- The acne is affecting your confidence or mood

Skin lightening creams and strong mixtures sold without guidance can damage the skin. Please have a doctor advise you before using them.
