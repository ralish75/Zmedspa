FAQ

Questions patients often ask

If your question is not here, message us on WhatsApp.

Do I need an appointment?

Yes. Please request one on the booking page and we will confirm a time with you on WhatsApp.

What are the clinic hours?

Monday to Saturday, 11:00 am to 7:00 pm.

Can I have an online consultation?

Yes. Choose "Online" on the booking form and we will confirm the details with you on WhatsApp. Some problems need an in-person examination, and we will tell you if that is the case.

Where is the clinic?

Zmedspa (MZ Derma Clinics), Klimmat Plaza, Asba and Dantata Street, Gwarinpa 2, Abuja.

How much does a consultation or treatment cost?

Costs depend on the problem and the treatment. Message us on WhatsApp or ask at booking and we will give you a clear figure before you commit.

What should I bring to my first visit?

Any creams, tablets or supplements you use, earlier test results, and a list of your questions. See the article on what to expect at your first visit.

Do laser and aesthetic treatments need more than one session?

Often yes. The number of sessions depends on the treatment and your skin, and we explain this at your consultation.

Will my information be kept private?

Yes. Your details are used only to arrange and carry out your care.

Ready to book?

Still unsure? Send a message and we will help you choose.
