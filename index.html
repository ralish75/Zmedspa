About the doctor

Meet your dermatosurgeon

Dr. Ismaila Kishimi is a medical doctor (MBBS) from the Faculty of Medicine, Ahmadu Bello University, with a postgraduate diploma in family medicine from the National Postgraduate Medical College of Nigeria. He earned an MSc in Dermatology and Dermatosurgery at the Institute of Dermatology, Bangkok, Thailand, where he spent three years training in dermatology, dermatosurgery and aesthetic techniques, and went on to fellowship training in allergic and occupational dermatology and in laser and cutaneous surgery at the same institute. At Zmedspa, the team treats conditions of the skin, hair and nails and explains every diagnosis and treatment plan in plain language, so you know what to expect.
