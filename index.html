About me

Meet your dermatologist

I am a dermatologist with an MSc in Dermatology and Dermatosurgery. I am currently a clinical fellow in allergic and occupational dermatology, laser and cutaneous surgery at the Institute of Dermatology in Bangkok, Thailand. I treat conditions of the skin, hair and nails, and I explain every diagnosis and treatment plan in plain language so you know what to expect.
