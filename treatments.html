Treatments

What we treat and how we can help

Medical, aesthetic, laser and surgical dermatology under one roof.

Skin

Skin concerns are the most common reason people see a dermatologist. After examining your skin, we explain what is causing the problem and set out a treatment plan you can follow at home.

  • Acne
  • Atopic dermatitis (eczema)
  • Seborrheic dermatitis
  • Skin infections and infestations

Whether a treatment is right for you, how many sessions it needs and the cost are all discussed at your consultation.

Hair

Hair loss has many possible causes. A careful history and examination, with simple tests where needed, help find the cause so treatment is aimed at the right problem.

  • Alopecias, scarring and non-scarring
  • Hair mount for hair analysis and diagnosis
  • Scalp biopsy where needed

Whether a treatment is right for you, how many sessions it needs and the cost are all discussed at your consultation.

Book a consultation

Nails

Nail problems can be slow to treat, so an early and accurate diagnosis matters. We look at the nail, explain the cause and discuss realistic timelines, and we perform nail surgery in the clinic.

  • Nail infections
  • Ingrown toenail surgery
  • Dystrophic nail surgery
  • Nail bed and matrix biopsies
  • Partial and complete nail avulsion with matricectomy
  • Lister’s granuloma surgery
  • Pincer nail surgery with flap reconstruction
  • Glomus tumour removal
  • Subungual foreign body removal

Whether a treatment is right for you, how many sessions it needs and the cost are all discussed at your consultation.

Book a consultation

Aesthetic medicine

Aesthetic treatments begin with a consultation, where we talk about what you would like to change and which options suit your skin.

  • Botulinum toxin
  • Hyaluronic acid dermal fillers (volumising)
  • Collagen biostimulators (PLLA, CAHA)
  • Skin boosters (Pink Glow, Amber Glow, PDRN, exosomes)
  • Microneedling, with actives or PRP
  • PRP injections

Whether a treatment is right for you, how many sessions it needs and the cost are all discussed at your consultation.

Chemical peels

Peels are chosen for your skin type and concern. We explain the number of sessions and how to care for your skin afterwards.

  • Brightening and depigmenting peels (Melanostop, BSK, Cosmelan)
  • Resurfacing peels, superficial and medium depth with TCA
  • Body peels for keratosis pilaris
  • Acne-specific peels (salicylic acid, azelan)
  • TCA CROSS for acne scars

Whether a treatment is right for you, how many sessions it needs and the cost are all discussed at your consultation.

Laser and hair removal

Laser treatments are planned around your skin type and goals. Most need more than one session, and we explain the number of sessions and the aftercare at your consultation.

  • CO2 laser for skin lesions: dermatosis papulosa nigra (DPN), warts, seborrheic keratosis, syringoma and sebaceous gland hyperplasia
  • CO2 laser resurfacing
  • Pigment removal laser (1064 nm Q-switched Nd:YAG)
  • Hair removal laser (1064 nm long-pulsed Nd:YAG)
  • Electrolysis for permanent hair removal
  • Carbon peeling

Whether a treatment is right for you, how many sessions it needs and the cost are all discussed at your consultation.

Cutaneous surgery and biopsies

Minor skin procedures are done in the clinic. We explain the procedure, the aftercare and what to expect before we begin.

  • Excisional and incisional biopsies of skin lesions
  • Punch biopsies of skin, scalp and nails
  • Shave biopsies
  • Lipoma excision
  • Inclusion and steatocystoma cyst excision
  • Removal of skin tags, seborrheic keratosis, xanthelasma, syringoma and sebaceous gland hypertrophy with CO2 laser or electrocautery
  • Wart removal (genital, plantar and body) with CO2 laser, electrocautery or cryotherapy

Whether a treatment is right for you, how many sessions it needs and the cost are all discussed at your consultation.

Scar and keloid treatment

Scars need a plan matched to the type of scar and your skin. We explain the options, how many sessions to expect and the aftercare.

  • Keloid injections
  • Cryotherapy for keloids
  • Keloid excision
  • Acne scar treatment: subcision, punch elevation, punch excision, TCA CROSS and CO2 laser resurfacing
  • Tribal mark revision surgery

Whether a treatment is right for you, how many sessions it needs and the cost are all discussed at your consultation.

Cryotherapy

Cryotherapy uses extreme cold to treat selected skin growths. We explain what to expect during and after treatment.

  • Intralesional cryotherapy for keloids
  • Cryotherapy for plantar and palmar warts
  • Skin tags and seborrheic keratosis

Whether a treatment is right for you, how many sessions it needs and the cost are all discussed at your consultation.

Allergic and occupational dermatology

Some skin problems are triggered by something you touch at home or at work. Finding the trigger is often the key to clearing the problem.

  • Contact allergy and patch testing
  • Work-related skin disease
  • Hand eczema

Whether a treatment is right for you, how many sessions it needs and the cost are all discussed at your consultation.

  • Dermoscopy
  • Wood’s lamp examination
  • Hair mount for hair analysis and diagnosis
  • Skin scrapings for KOH microscopy
