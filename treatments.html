What I treat and how I can help

Skin

Skin concerns are the most common reason people see a dermatologist. After examining your skin, I explain what is causing the problem and set out a treatment plan you can follow at home.

  • Acne and acne scars
  • Eczema and dermatitis
  • Psoriasis, fungal and other skin infections

Whether a treatment is right for you, how many sessions it needs and the cost are all discussed at your consultation.

Hair

Hair loss and scalp problems have many possible causes. A careful history and examination help find the cause so treatment is aimed at the right problem.

  • Hair loss
  • Dandruff and scalp conditions
  • Scalp infections

Whether a treatment is right for you, how many sessions it needs and the cost are all discussed at your consultation.

Nails

Nail problems can be slow to treat, so an early and accurate diagnosis matters. I look at the nail, explain the cause and discuss realistic timelines.

  • Fungal nail infection
  • Nail psoriasis and other nail disorders
  • Ingrown nails

Whether a treatment is right for you, how many sessions it needs and the cost are all discussed at your consultation.
